I work on variational quantum algorithms, parametrized circuit characterization, and measurement protocols
for near-term systems at [NeoQuortex](https://www.neoquortex.com/). My work sits at the intersection of quantum
chemistry and software engineering: taking research-level methods and turning them into extensible, benchmarked
frameworks. Before quantum, I spent two years as a backend engineer shipping production telecom systems at Amdocs.

**Currently building:**
- ADAPT-VQE from scratch in PennyLane across 9 operator pools, benchmarked on molecular systems from H₂ (4q) to N₂ (20q)
- A modular circuit-characterization framework showcasing expressibility, entanglement capability, and compiled gate depth for arbitrary ansatze
- A pluggable measurement module supporting qubit-wise commuting, classical shadows, and fermionic shadows

**Focus:** variational algorithms · quantum chemistry · quantum circuit characterization ·
measurement protocols · PennyLane
