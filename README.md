## 🌌 Who Am I? (A Superposition of Things)

A **Quantum Software Engineer & MS grad in Quantum Information Science** (USC '25), which basically means I've chosen to make my career in a field where the fundamental unit of information can be *both* 0 and 1 — and I find this deeply comforting as a person who can never pick one favourite anything.

Presently, as a **Quantum Software Engineer at [NeoQuortex](https://www.neoquortex.com/)**, I'm doing some exciting early-stage work at the intersection of quantum chemistry, quantum circuits, algorithms, and software (more details to come when we're out in the wild! 🚀).

Before quantum called my name, I spent two years as a **Software Developer at Amdocs** shipping telecom solutions — working with Java, APIs, MySQL, production releases, and client calls — the whole nine yards. Turns out understanding *classical* computing makes quantum computing feel even more beautifully, chaotically entangled.

Beyond software, I've taught high schoolers that quantum computing is cool, AR/VR on Meta Quest headsets is cooler, and semiconductors are the coolest - the unsung heroes of the modern world (that's my FAITH). I've also experimented with hardware and electronics all my life — and would love to break something apart to see how it works, just like I did with my first 3x3 Rubik's cube. Moreover, I spent my entire undergrad journey stargazing with an astronomy society, and space exploration, along with the quest to uncover the secrets of the universe, remain among my greatest loves. I like to think of myself as a person of science in every aspect of life — even while cooking, this curious brain will simply not turn off.

Organised, neat, dedicated — and genuinely excited to take on whatever comes next. ✨
